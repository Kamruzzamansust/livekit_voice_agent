TRIP PLAN: KHULNA AND THE SUNDARBANS (4 DAYS, 3 NIGHTS)

== Overview ==
Khulna is the third largest city of Bangladesh and the main gateway to the Sundarbans, the largest mangrove forest in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is the home of the Royal Bengal Tiger, spotted deer, saltwater crocodiles and hundreds of bird species. Near Khulna is Bagerhat, the historic Mosque City with the famous Sixty Dome Mosque (Shat Gombuj Masjid), also a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Khulna is also famous for chui jhal mutton and fresh golda chingri (giant river prawn).

== Best Time to Visit ==
November to February is the best time, with cool dry weather and calm rivers, ideal for the Sundarbans boat trip. March to May is hot. June to September is monsoon with rough rivers, and the forest is closed for tourism from June to August for the breeding season.

== How to Get There ==
By bus from Dhaka: via the Padma Bridge, about 5 to 6 hours, 700 to 1,500 BDT.
By train from Dhaka: Sundarban Express or Chitra Express, about 6 to 9 hours, 500 to 1,800 BDT.
By air: fly from Dhaka to Jessore (about 40 minutes), then 1.5 hours by road to Khulna.

== Day-by-Day Itinerary ==
Day 1: Travel from Dhaka to Khulna in the morning. Afternoon day trip to Bagerhat (about 1 hour by road): visit the Sixty Dome Mosque, the shrine of Khan Jahan Ali and the Singair Mosque. Return to Khulna, evening at Rupsha Bridge, and dinner with chui jhal mutton.
Day 2: Board a Sundarbans tour launch (cruise boat) from Khulna early in the morning. Cruise through the Rupsha and Pasur rivers. Afternoon visit to Harbaria eco-trail with a wooden walkway through the mangroves. Night on the launch in the forest.
Day 3: Early morning canal ride on a small country boat to see deer, monkeys, kingfishers and, if lucky, tiger pugmarks. Visit Kotka beach and watch tower, then Jamtola sea beach. Walk the forest trail with an armed forest guard. Night on the launch.
Day 4: Visit Karamjal wildlife and crocodile breeding centre near Mongla, then return to Khulna by afternoon and travel back to Dhaka.

== Sundarbans Tour Packages ==
A standard 3 day / 2 night Sundarbans package from Khulna costs about 9,000 to 18,000 BDT per person. It usually includes the launch cabin, all meals, forest entry permits, an armed forest guard and a guide. Day trips to Karamjal only cost about 1,500 to 3,000 BDT. Entry into the forest requires a permit from the Forest Department; licensed tour operators arrange this, so always book with a registered operator.

== Where to Stay in Khulna ==
Budget hotels: 1,500 to 3,000 BDT per night.
Mid-range hotels: 3,500 to 6,000 BDT per night.
Upscale hotels: 7,000 to 12,000 BDT per night.

== Food ==
Must-try: chui jhal mutton or beef (cooked with chui pepper vine), golda chingri malai curry, hilsa, crab, and Khulna's sweets. A good meal costs about 300 to 800 BDT per person.

== Estimated Budget (per person, from Dhaka) ==
Budget trip: about 14,000 to 18,000 BDT.
Comfortable trip: about 22,000 to 30,000 BDT.
This includes return transport from Dhaka, one hotel night in Khulna, the Sundarbans package and food.

== Packing List ==
Light long-sleeved clothes in neutral colours (green, brown, khaki), a light jacket for cold river nights in winter, mosquito repellent, sunscreen, cap, binoculars, comfortable shoes for mud and trails, a power bank, a copy of your NID or passport (needed for the forest permit) and personal medicines.

== Rules and Safety Tips ==
Never leave the group or the forest trail without the armed forest guard; the Sundarbans has wild tigers and crocodiles.
Do not swim in the rivers or canals.
Keep quiet in the forest to see more wildlife; do not feed animals.
Plastic and littering are banned inside the forest.
Mobile network is weak or absent deep inside the forest; inform family before departure.
