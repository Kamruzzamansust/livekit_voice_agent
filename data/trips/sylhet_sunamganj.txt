TRIP PLAN: SYLHET AND SUNAMGANJ (4 DAYS, 3 NIGHTS)

== Overview ==
Sylhet in north-eastern Bangladesh is known for green tea gardens, hills on the Indian border, crystal-clear rivers, and the holy shrine of Hazrat Shah Jalal. Its neighbouring district Sunamganj is the land of haors, huge wetlands that turn into an inland sea during the monsoon. The most famous is Tanguar Haor, a Ramsar wetland of international importance, where travellers stay overnight on houseboats.

== Best Time to Visit ==
For Tanguar Haor, Ratargul swamp forest and the river spots, the best time is the monsoon and just after it, from June to October, when the water is high. Tea gardens are green all year. In winter (November to February) the haor water goes down and it becomes a destination for migratory birds. In February the Shimul Bagan (red silk cotton tree garden) near Tahirpur blooms. Avoid flash-flood warnings in the peak rainy season.

== How to Get There ==
By bus from Dhaka to Sylhet: about 6 to 7 hours, 800 to 1,600 BDT.
By train from Dhaka to Sylhet: Parabat Express, Joyontika Express or Upaban Express (night train), about 7 to 8 hours, 400 to 1,500 BDT.
By air: Dhaka to Sylhet (Osmani International Airport) in about 45 minutes, 4,500 to 8,000 BDT one way.
From Sylhet to Sunamganj town: about 2 hours by bus or CNG auto-rickshaw. From Sunamganj town to Tahirpur (the starting point for Tanguar Haor): about 1.5 hours by road or motorbike.

== Day-by-Day Itinerary ==
Day 1 (Sylhet city and tea gardens): Arrive in Sylhet by the night train. Visit the shrines of Hazrat Shah Jalal and Hazrat Shah Paran, then Malnicherra Tea Estate (one of the oldest tea gardens in the subcontinent) and Lakkatura Tea Garden. Evening at Keane Bridge and Ali Amjad's Clock on the Surma River.
Day 2 (Jaflong and Ratargul): Morning trip to Jaflong (about 2 hours), where the Piyain river flows from the Meghalaya hills with stone beds and clear water; also see Mayabi waterfall area and the Khasi village. On the way back, visit Ratargul Swamp Forest by small boat (the only freshwater swamp forest of Bangladesh; best in monsoon). Optional alternatives: Lalakhal for its blue-green river water, or Bisnakandi and Sada Pathor (Bholaganj) for white stones and hills.
Day 3 (Sunamganj and Tanguar Haor): Early morning travel to Sunamganj, then Tahirpur. Board a houseboat for Tanguar Haor. Swim or ride a small boat near the watch tower and the hijol-koroch swamp trees, enjoy the view of the Meghalaya hills, and have lunch with fresh haor fish on board. Sleep on the houseboat on the haor.
Day 4 (Niladri Lake and return): Morning visit to Niladri Lake (Shahid Siraj Lake) at Takerghat, a blue lake from old limestone quarries, then Barikka Tila hill and the Jadukata river view. In February, add the Shimul Bagan. Return to Sunamganj, then Sylhet, and take the night train or bus to Dhaka.

== Houseboat and Tour Packages ==
Tanguar Haor houseboat trips (1 night, 2 days) range from about 4,000 to 12,000 BDT per person depending on the boat class, usually including all meals and boat travel. Booking a full houseboat for a group of 8 to 12 people is cheaper per person. Book weeks ahead in the July to September season. Sylhet day trips by CNG cost about 2,000 to 3,500 BDT per vehicle per day; a microbus is about 4,000 to 6,000 BDT per day.

== Where to Stay in Sylhet ==
Budget hotels near Zindabazar and Amberkhana: 1,200 to 3,000 BDT per night.
Mid-range hotels: 3,500 to 7,000 BDT per night.
Tea-garden resorts and luxury resorts: 10,000 to 30,000 BDT per night.

== Food ==
Must-try: Sylheti satkora beef or fish curry (cooked with a local citrus called satkora), shutki bhorta, haor fish on the houseboat, fresh Sylhet tea and Panshi or Pach Bhai restaurants' Bengali meals. A meal costs about 250 to 700 BDT per person.

== Estimated Budget (per person, from Dhaka) ==
Budget trip: about 10,000 to 14,000 BDT.
Comfortable trip: about 18,000 to 28,000 BDT.
This includes return train or bus from Dhaka, one hotel night in Sylhet, one houseboat night, local transport and food.

== Packing List ==
Rain coat or umbrella, quick-dry clothes, waterproof bag for the phone, sandals with grip, life jacket (most houseboats provide one, but check), mosquito repellent, power bank, sunglasses, sunscreen and basic medicine.

== Rules and Safety Tips ==
Always wear a life jacket on boats in the haor and rivers; water is deep and currents can be strong in monsoon.
Jaflong and Bisnakandi are next to the Indian border; do not cross the border markers.
Do not throw plastic or food packets into Tanguar Haor; it is a protected wetland.
Check weather and flash-flood alerts before going to the haor in the peak monsoon.
Swimming in Ratargul is not allowed because of snakes and deep water.
